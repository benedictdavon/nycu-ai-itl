Western blot for a 150 kDa protein, following the lab western blot protocol. The wet transfer at 100 V for 1 hour failed for this protein: the signal at the expected size was almost absent, and the protein seemed to stay in the gel. Fixed by a longer transfer at lower voltage: 30 V overnight in the cold room. The signal at the expected size was clear after that. Blocking and antibody steps were unchanged.
